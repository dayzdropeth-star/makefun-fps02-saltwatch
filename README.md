SALTWATCH is a round-based tactical FPS built with three.js: two teams fight short rounds in the Salt Dock at night, win by elimination or by planting and detonating the charge, earn money between rounds, and spend it on rifles, SMGs, a sniper and grenades. Gunplay rewards stopping to aim — spread and recoil punish firing on the move, crouching tightens your shots and quiets your steps, and the enemy bots hear your gunfire and come looking. Single-player against bots, no network needed.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8080 --directory game
```

Open http://localhost:8080, click to play. WASD to move, mouse to aim, click to fire, R to reload, B to buy, E to plant/defuse, Ctrl to crouch, Shift to sprint.
